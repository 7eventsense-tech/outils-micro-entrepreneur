2026 quarterly estimated tax due dates (and how much to pay)

If you're self-employed, a freelancer or a 1099 contractor, nobody withholds tax from your income — you pay it to the IRS in four installments (Form 1040-ES). Here are the dates for the 2026 tax year and a quick calculator.

PaymentIncome earnedDue date
Q1Jan 1 – Mar 31, 2026April 15, 2026
Q2Apr 1 – May 31, 2026June 15, 2026
Q3Jun 1 – Aug 31, 2026September 15, 2026
Q4Sep 1 – Dec 31, 2026January 15, 2027

If a date falls on a weekend or legal holiday, the deadline moves to the next business day. Many states have their own estimated-tax schedule.

How much should I pay each quarter?

Safe-harbor rules (avoid the underpayment penalty)

You generally avoid the IRS underpayment penalty if your 2026 payments (estimated + withholding) are at least the smaller of: 90 % of your 2026 tax, or 100 % of your 2025 tax (110 % if your 2025 AGI was above $150,000). You also owe no penalty if you'll owe less than $1,000 after withholding and credits.
